2026 CEO FIRESIDES

The CEOs unscripted. On the record.

Janine Sullivan

President & CEO, FortisAlberta

Janine brings nearly 30 years of industry experience to her role as President and Chief Executive Officer of FortisAlberta. A longtime member of the… Read moreFortis group of companies, she has developed a deep understanding of the regulated utility space in progressive leadership positions at both Newfoundland Power and FortisAlberta. As President and CEO, Janine leads the company's efforts to safely deliver reliable, affordable and sustainable electricity to more than half a million customers across Alberta. Under her direction, FortisAlberta is fostering innovation, investing in communities and supporting economic growth across its service territory. A respected voice in Canada's energy landscape, Janine received the King Charles III Coronation Medal in recognition of her leadership and ongoing support of women and youth pursuing careers in the skilled trades. She was named an inaugural member of Electricity Canada's Women in Electricity Leadership Circle and is a sought-after panelist and speaker at industry events. Janine is a Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA, CA) in Alberta and Newfoundland and Labrador, and holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) from Memorial University. She serves on the Board of Directors of FortisBC, Electricity Canada, Western Energy Institute and CAREERS: The Next Generation, where she supports initiatives that connect youth with career opportunities in the skilled trades. Originally from Newfoundland, Janine has proudly called Alberta home for almost 20 years. She has come to know the province well — by both regularly visiting the company's 37 service points and driving her two boys to hockey rinks across Alberta. Read less

Robert Hayes

President & CEO, Calgary Sports & Entertainment Corporation

Robert Hayes has been President and CEO of Calgary Sports & Entertainment Corporation since 2024. He represents CSEC's ownership group in overseeing… Read moreall business operations of five sports teams, music, comedy, entertainment and related community supporting Foundations. Mr. Hayes had a 30+ year career in the financial sector, working throughout Canada and the US with the last 20 years in Calgary. He has held numerous Senior Executive positions, primarily leading client facing teams focused on areas such as mergers & acquisitions, corporate finance, sports & entertainment, private banking, risk management, and international. A native of Halifax, Mr. Hayes holds a Bachelor of Commerce degree from Dalhousie University, FCMA, FCPA, and ICD.D designations. He has completed Executive Leadership & Strategic Management programs at Northwestern University, University of Western Ontario, and University of Toronto. In the community, Mr. Hayes presently sits on the Calgary Flames Foundation, Calgary Stampeders Foundation, Calgary Economic Development, Calgary Film Centre, and Prostate Cancer Centre Boards. Historically, he has served on the Calgary Stampede Foundation's $100 million capital campaign committee, served three terms on the United Way Executive Campaign Cabinet, chaired the Wood's Homes Foundation Board that included a Capital Campaign and CEO search, was a Westside Recreation Centre board member chairing the Human Resource Committee, and was a member of the Peter Lougheed Centre Development Council for over 10 years. In 2013, Mr. Hayes was recognized as one of Alberta's 50 Most Influential People. Read less

Mark Little

Founder & CEO, Jotson (Former President & CEO, Suncor)

Mark Little is the Founder and CEO of Jotson Inc., a technology platform empowering Canadian households and businesses to manage energy costs and… Read moreconsumption. Mark has more than 35 years of leadership experience in the Canadian energy industry, including 23 years at Imperial/Exxon, and most recently as President & CEO at Suncor Energy (2019-2022). During his career, Mark has led large complex organizations working on multi-billion-dollar energy projects, from the conceptual design, development, and project implementation through to operations and maintenance. Mark has led the startup and operation of large wind, solar, and biogas projects, and is one of the founding CEOs of Pathways Alliance, an initiative that sets the six largest oil sands companies in Canada on a path to reach net-zero CO2 emissions. He is a board member of General Fusion, an advisor to the Coalition for a Better Future, and is the 2024 recipient of the CCIB Award for Excellence in Aboriginal Relations. Mark holds two degrees – one in computer science from the University of Calgary, and one in applied petroleum engineering technology from the Southern Alberta Institute of Technology. He is also a graduate of the advanced management program at Harvard Business School. Read less

Who This Room Is For

The room of 150 is curated so every conversation counts.

Who This Is For

  • Rising executives 1–2 levels from the C-Suite
  • Operators carrying real P&L, team, or organizational responsibility
  • Leaders committed to building capacity, sharpening judgment, and lengthening their leadership runway
  • Cross-industry collaborators who value the room over the pitch
  • Returning Champions Summit alumni and peers nominated by them

Who This Is Not For

  • Service providers, consultants, or coaches treating the event as prospecting
  • Sales professionals using the room to build pipeline
  • Leaders more than two levels from the C-Suite (we'll point you to other Level 52 programs that fit)
  • Anyone whose primary intent is selling rather than building
THE CHAMPIONS CODE

This is not a conference. It is a standard.

Champions Summit exists to forge a community of current and future executives committed to responsibility over status, contribution over consumption, and long-term impact over short-term wins — for Calgary and beyond.

  • 01We choose responsibility over status.
  • 02We speak what matters, not what's safe.
  • 03We contribute before we consume.
  • 04We build beyond ourselves.
  • 05We play the long game.

How we lead in this room is how we lead everywhere.
